# REG frontier research — decision memo

Date: 2026-11-04
Worktree: tk2-reg-position-failure-mechanism-v1
Decision: **NONE_JUSTIFIED**

## Decision

No new REG model lane is admitted from this read-only review. Several exact variants remain unrun or conditional, but the plausible mechanisms are already represented by executed pilots, diagnostics, or explicit speculative lanes. The unrun variants lack a distinct evidence-backed reason to spend another fit budget on the same repeatedly reused Train outcomes.

This decision closes the current frontier while recognizing that some variants remain unrun. A REG-specific learned second-order interaction and a new past-only residual head have no exact completed pilot identified here. They already appear in ResearchNext or overlap mechanisms with failed, diagnostic-only, or technically unsupported evidence.

## R06 comparator and evaluation boundary

The frozen R06 recipe uses char_wb TF-IDF character n-grams 3–5, min_df 3, cap 30,000, fit-only structured/OHE features and a missing-stream indicator, LightGBM regression_l1, and a fixed policy of 75% incumbent plus 25% within-cycle rank50 of raw R06, clipped to [0,100]. The recipe records two STANDARD predictor origins, 55 and 67, with separately learned preprocessing per cutoff. Evidence: experiments/optimization_v3/batch1/R06/recipe_revision_02.json, recipe keys at lines 5–34, objective at line 52, vectorizer at lines 70–80; reports/optimization_v3/batch1/R06/summary.md:5–17.

For the saved W6 comparison, the reported R06 MAE is 11.653450 at E67 and 9.921613 at E75. Both views score cycles 76–79 on 8,491 rows; E67 has training cutoff 67 and E75 cutoff 75. The origins differ but use the same already-scored outcome rows, so this is adaptive development evidence. The E75 value is the saved later-origin R06 comparator reported in the R-NBR and position pilots; the R06 revision_02 recipe itself records only origins 55 and 67. Evidence: reports/optimization_v3/rnbr_pilot_v1/official_execution_v1/RESULTS.md:23–27; reports/optimization_v3/reg_position_l1_v1/FINDINGS.md:9–16.

## Strongest counterevidence

There is residual signal in isolated views. R-NBR gained 0.071990 MAE on its primary view and 0.179576 at E75 W6, but lost 0.048271 on H12 and remained diagnostic-only. The post-hoc present-only R12 policy reported primary/H12 gains of 0.285422/0.306170, and it was chosen after those labels were inspected. Treat it as possible signal without independent confirmation or authority to continue. Evidence: reports/optimization_v3/rnbr_pilot_v1/official_execution_v1/RESULTS.md:15–30; reports/optimization_v3/postscreen_review/REVIEW.md:53–63.

Position evidence is weaker as a new direction: the direct-position audit reports that its E75 W6 saved policy harms R06 for every tested arm and that the broad rank50 blend and residual-position transport families already cover the nearest approaches. Evidence: reports/optimization_v3/reg_position_failure_mechanism_v1/MECHANISM_AUDIT.md:27–43.

## Repeated-development bias and stop

The 45-card catalog is a proposal inventory. Reconciliation records terminal pilot decisions for 43/45 cards, with R10/R13 as explicit prerequisite skips. Primary and H12 reuse Train outcomes; the research plan explicitly warns that more recipe selection can optimize validation noise. ResearchNext reports no model continuation eligible under its queue rules. Evidence: reports/optimization_v3/research_backlog/RESEARCH_PLAN.md:7,57–67; reports/optimization_v3/batch1/RECONCILIATION_01.md:39; reports/optimization_v3/research_next_v1/CAMPAIGN_SUMMARY.md:7,42.

Technical preflight: no candidate enters a new fit. The named learned-FM direction still requires a backend that supports the intended L1 objective, gradient/numerical checks, and a resource/serialization preflight; ResearchNext records dependency feasibility as incomplete. No dependency was installed and no model was fitted for this review. Evidence: reports/optimization_v3/research_next_v1/RESEARCH_LANES.md:119–125,145–150.

Scientific stop: close this frontier at **zero new predictor fits and zero learned-preprocessing fits**. E67/E75 overlap remains development support and cannot serve as a fresh holdout. No E79 run, Test/Sample/Kaggle review, or rescue tuning on these same labels follows from this memo. Reopen only with a separately justified mechanism and a frozen recipe, comparator, matched controls, gates, and fit accounting before any new outcomes are examined.
