## Decision context

The verified internal incumbent is the fixed three-component recipe:

`0.5625 × LGB SVD32(rank50) + 0.1875 × Hybrid Ridge(rank75) + 0.25 × sparse LGB(rank50)`, clipped to `[0,100]`.

Its row-pooled primary MAE is **11.559390** on 38,588 rows; H12 MAE is **14.047533** on the same 38,588 Train records under two fixed origins. The accepted public Kaggle result is **11.35970**, reference **57835122**. Public score is a separate benchmark and must not select experiments. Primary/H12 reuse the same known Train outcomes and are adaptive development evidence, not independent or untouched holdouts.

Frozen full-six promotion gates already used in V2 are a useful unchanged default: primary MAE gain at least `0.03`; both H12_E and H12_L harm no greater than `0.05`; at least 4/6 primary windows non-worse; and no supported (n≥100) tail or missing-stream MAE harm above `0.25`. Keep the exact current champion as the paired control. Do not relax these gates after viewing results.

For a cheap family screen, use only two already-declared origins, cutoff 55 and cutoff 67. This gives W1+W4 primary support (9,064 rows) and H12_E/H12_L views (38,588 pooled rows). W1+W4 is a *pilot*, not the full primary score. Advance a family only if its frozen candidate policy improves pooled W1+W4 by at least `0.03` MAE, neither W1 nor W4 is worse by more than `0.25`, H12_E and H12_L harm are each at most `0.05`, and supported tail/missing harm is at most `0.25` for n≥100. A full-six continuation reuses the two pilot fits and adds only cutoffs 59, 63, 71, and 75. Report raw and equal-cycle MAE, each cycle/window, signed bias, fixed target tails, EF0/EF1, signal availability, category novelty, and group denominators. All learned transformations and fit-defined cut points must be past-only.

## What the evidence says, and what it does not

- `reports/optimization_v3/regression_diagnostics/FINDINGS.md` and `diagnostics.json` reconstruct the incumbent from row-exact saved predictions. Repeated diagnostics show EF1−EF0 mean residual positive in 6/6 folds, unseen org/module/pattern versus seen positive in 6/6, and low-tail minus high-tail mean residual positive in 6/6. Stream-missing minus present is positive in 5/6. Tail rows have strong asymmetric shrinkage: low targets are overpredicted and high targets underpredicted. These are development diagnostics, not proven causes or deployable routes.
- Global time bias changes direction between H12_E (`+1.333`) and H12_L (`−1.296`). That supports bounded temporal robustness checks, but does not imply a future correction will transfer.
- Several attractive ideas have already failed and should stay retired: the ten-bin CDF median worsened pilot MAE by `+0.733931`; its fixed 25% blend did not clear the `0.03` pilot margin. The OOF nonlinear residual head worsened pooled W1+W4 by `0.086814` and H12 by `0.080881`. Character Ridge improved H12 by `0.130071` but worsened six-window primary by `0.158371`, with 57/365 supported subgroup comparisons exceeding `0.25` harm. Ordered token summary features worsened the fixed blend by `0.116570` on primary pilot and `0.128519` on H12. The zero-fit `median3` policy failed; `median25` gained only `0.013118`, below the frozen margin. See `reports/optimization_v3/FINDINGS.md`, `regression_distributional/`, `regression_residual/`, `regression_character_full/`, `supervised_pilot/FULLPILOT.md`, and `regression_robust_aggregate/`.
- R1/R2 and V1/V2 already covered structured HGB and feature/history ablations; Ridge/SGD and LightGBM; text-only/hybrid Ridge; HGB+SVD32/128; native tabular LightGBM; word TF-IDF sparse L1 LightGBM; rank50/rank75 transforms; CatBoost MAE; and blend weight selection. Do not reopen generic feature removals, a new SVD-width sweep, ordinary CatBoost MAE, SGD L1, unigram-vs-bigram classifier ablations, or convex-weight grids. Audit map: `docs/REGRESSION_RESEARCH.md`, `docs/REGRESSION_ROUND2.md`, `reports/regression_research/FINDINGS.md`, `reports/optimization_v2/FINDINGS.md`, and `reports/dual_campaign/regression/`.
- The ordered-token pilot averaged token-history summaries and lost token identity. A different identity-preserving, strictly past-only selection design remains a low-priority hypothesis, not a continuation of that failed summary model. `reports/optimization_v3/research_backlog/feasibility.json` reports 93,338 distinct lowercase word unigrams in the **Classification** Train format. This only motivates checking vocabulary representation feasibility; it is a separate target and is not evidence about Regression `threat_rank`.
- The previous SGD sparse-L1 recipes reached the fixed 2,000-iteration cap with convergence warnings. This is concrete evidence for testing a different linear solver on the *same sparse design*, while clearly treating it as solver/regularization comparison rather than a new loss family.

## Recommended queue for Luna

Luna applies each card's advance/retire/support gates without waiting for per-ID Sol review; MAIN reviews the receipts and report after the batch. ## Runtime and execution limits

Measured comparable Regression sparse-LGB fits in `reports/optimization_v2/regression_repr/fullsix.json` took **17.28–56.51 seconds** for 16,134–42,117 fit rows. The 2-fit pilot should therefore be roughly minutes of estimator time for a similar sparse LGB pipeline, but feature construction and verifier/report work add time. The older SGD fits took **20.65–79.72 seconds** and all hit their 2,000-iteration cap. Ordered-token-summary fits took **3.64–9.10 seconds**, but the direct identity expansion here has unknown width and cost. Existing SVD32 LGB runtime, NMF, LinearSVR convergence, rankers, ordinal models, and character LGB are **not measured**; use fit counts only until a same-machine receipt is available. Ordinal is nine heads per fold, so budget roughly nine single-head model fits, not one.

Use the adjacent `execution_protocol_draft.md` for the remaining controls: single fit pipeline at a time, two LightGBM threads, prefit path/schema/dry-run, freeze exact source/input/recipe/fold identities before candidate scores, atomic exclusive receipts, independent verifier, and serial continuation only through the preassigned unattended queue. ## Official implementation references

- LightGBM documents `regression_l1`, `huber`, `fair`, `lambdarank`, and ranking relevance `label_gain`; `lambdarank` relevance labels must be integer indices covered by `label_gain`. Set `label_gain` explicitly to a linear list for this task rather than LightGBM's exponential default, because the competition target is continuous and primary evaluation is MAE. The ranker's ordering objective still does not optimize MAE. [LightGBM 4.6 parameters](https://lightgbm.readthedocs.io/en/v4.6.0/Parameters.html)
- LightGBM's `linear_tree` does not support `regression_l1`; do not pair `linear_tree=true` with the current L1 objective. [LightGBM linear-tree limitation](https://lightgbm.readthedocs.io/en/v4.6.0/Parameters.html)
- A single `quantile(alpha=0.5)` run is the same conditional-quantile target functional as absolute-error regression, so it is not a new family by itself. The proposal intentionally does not spend a card on that repetition. [LightGBM quantile objective](https://lightgbm.readthedocs.io/en/v4.6.0/Parameters.html)
- CatBoost documents groups and YetiRank pair generation/objectives; keep integer cycle groups and integer relevance labels explicit. This does not guarantee MAE alignment. [CatBoost ranking objectives](https://catboost.ai/docs/en/concepts/loss-functions-ranking), [CatBoostRanker fit](https://catboost.ai/docs/en/concepts/python-reference_catboostranker_fit)
