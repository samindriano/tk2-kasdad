# Retrospective findings: local gates and public outcomes

**Decision.** Prior `STOP` / `NONE_JUSTIFIED` language was too broad if read as “no predictive value” or “scientifically exhausted.” The evidence supports the narrower statement: no candidate met the original continuation queue's unchanged eligibility rules at that checkpoint. Preserve all historical verdicts; add these retrospective categories separately. A gate failure rejects its frozen recipe under its declared baseline, horizon, metric, and risk rule; it does not prove zero information for a whole mechanism family.

## 45-card review

The Nov 1 authoritative registry covers 20 Classification and 25 Regression cards. It records 43 terminal saved pilot decisions and two prerequisite skips, R10/R13; those skips are unmeasured. Cached six-origin outputs are evidence but not new predictor fits. Repaired technical attempts such as R04 do not make the final saved result inconclusive. The [45-card table](CARD_DISPOSITION.md) preserves original statuses and records mechanism, local gains, failure type, and exact saved evidence.

Retrospective categories are: (1) aggregate negative/near-null for a frozen recipe; (2) positive aggregate rejected by a risk or horizon guard; (3) prerequisite skip, therefore unmeasured; (4) post hoc route potential selected after results; (5) public benchmark observed, which is separate from local validation. Positive aggregate with a failed guard is not a champion, but is also not “no signal.” Conversely, skipped or untested variants are not positive evidence.

## Why local and public ranks differ

The card protocols have distinct denominators. STANDARD uses W1+W4 (n=9,064) and H12 (n=38,588); LATE uses W4+W6 (n=13,441) and H12_L (n=20,903); CACHE_SIX follows its own six-origin policy. Primary and H12 can reuse labels. E67 H12 scores cycles 68–79 after fitting through 67; E75 W6 scores 76–79 after fitting through 75, so those windows overlap. E79 deployment instead trains through 79 and predicts 80–91. Train provides no untouched 12-cycle future block after all 33 training cycles.

Comparators also change by stage: early Regression cards use legacy/R1-D; continuation uses R22 primary and R06 secondary; public comparisons now use R06 or current MIX50 depending on the task. Do not compare gains across these baselines as if they were one estimand. Development labels were reused across many candidates, and the best among repeated public scores is selected; public/private ranking, paired uncertainty, and causal explanations remain unknown.

## Counterevidence and stop point

Many recipes were genuinely adverse or below their materiality bar: C14, R07, R08 and R20 were strongly negative; recent Classification position-family public scores (CGEOM .82884, JointPos .82911, Moments .82979, Bigram .83064, Contrast .82935) remain below MIX50 .83144. R22 qualified internally but scored 11.30535 publicly versus R06 11.16620. Conversely, R06's strong local aggregate and best public score coexist with later missing-W1 harm +0.395556 MAE on n=392. A pooled scoring objective and a protected subgroup guard can rank the same model differently.

Read-only official CLI snapshots contain 19 Classification records (18 COMPLETE, one ERROR) and 18 Regression records (all COMPLETE). The ERROR row is the Classification `samuel_rnbr_final_e79.csv` entry; its Regression entry is COMPLETE. The reason for that Classification status is not inferred here. Public subset composition, private scores, and server-side hashes are unavailable. The snapshots are [`Classification`](../KAGGLE_CLASSIFICATION_SNAPSHOT_20261104.json) and [`Regression`](../KAGGLE_REGRESSION_SNAPSHOT_20261104.json); direct-position recipe equivalence remains with the separate REG audit.

**Checkpoint:** the campaign was not scientifically exhausted; it was stopped because no additional fit qualified under the frozen queue. This retrospective authorizes no fit, model change, Test-label access, or submission. Review the independent REG lineage report before deciding whether any future amendment is warranted.
